# 🕹️ USER MANUAL: SPECCY OS E5 ULTRA (V1.0.4 - IMPERIAL ERA)

Welcome to **Speccy OS**, the ultimate frontend designed from scratch to push your retro console hardware to its limit, with special optimization for modern chipsets and advanced control systems.

This manual will guide you through the system's basic and advanced features.

---

## 🚀 1. FIRST STEPS

### 1.1. Library Linking (Roms)
1. When starting the app for the first time, sign in with your Google account. (If your console doesn't have Google Services, use the integrated Web/Local access).
2. Follow the wizard to select your **Device DNA** (e.g., GameMT E5 Ultra, Anbernic, Retroid, etc.). This will calibrate telemetry and overclock precisely.
3. In the **"Link Library"** screen, select your storage's root folder.
4. **Multi-Path & Direct Folders:** You can now add multiple locations (internal SD, external USB) simultaneously. The system will recognize both general "Roms" folders and individual system folders (e.g., an SD containing only "psx").

### 1.2. Initial Scan (Zero-Scraping)
Speccy OS integrates revolutionary **Zero-Scraping** technology. The system will map all your existing `media` folder (images, videos, fanarts) in milliseconds and link them to your ROMs without downloading internet data.
**Parallel Scanning:** The new V1.0.4 engine scans all your storage paths at the same time, cutting waiting times in half.

## 🎮 2. NAVIGATION & CONTROLS

Speccy OS is designed to be used 100% with a physical Gamepad.

*   **D-Pad Left/Right:** Change System (Console).
*   **D-Pad Up/Down:** Navigate through the game list.
*   **Button A (or START):** Launch the selected game.
*   **Button B:** Go back.
*   **Button X:** Open the global game search.

Speccy OS includes a native AI called **The Architect**, aware of your installed games and trained with the *Nebula V2 Core*.

1.  Press the **ARCHITECT** button in the bottom control panel.
2.  Ask it for cheats, lore, or recommendations.

## ⚡ 4. IMPERIAL LINK & PERFORMANCE

Speccy OS dynamically controls your console's processor to save battery or provide maximum performance.

### 4.1. Shizuku Support (Imperial Link)
If your device is not rooted but you want full control over the hardware, you can now use **Shizuku**.
1. Go to **Settings > Device**.
2. Press **"Link with Shizuku"**.
3. Once active, Speccy OS will be able to change clock frequencies and manage the fan persistently, just as if you were a Root user.

*   **ECO Mode:** Maximum battery saving.
*   **BALANCED/PERFORMANCE Mode:** Balance for 16/32 bit systems.
*   **EXTREME Mode:** For demanding emulators (PS2, GameCube, Switch).

## 🎨 5. VISUAL CUSTOMIZATION (Themes & Neon)

### 5.1. Neon Color Lock
Do you like a specific color and don't want it to change depending on the console?
1. Enter **Settings > Interface**.
2. Disable the **"Auto-change by system"** option.
3. Choose your profile (Cyber, Acid, Magma, etc.). Now that color will be fixed across the entire Dashboard.

### 5.2. Dynamic Fanart
Choose between displaying **Cyberpunk Art** or **Iconic Characters** on the Dashboard's giant cards. You can also adapt the interface **Aspect Ratio** (16:9, 4:3, 1:1) to look perfect on any screen.

## ☁️ 6. NEBULA SYNC V2

Your save games are safe. If you are a **PRO** user, Speccy OS will upload your `.state` files to the Google Drive cloud automatically. You can start playing on your handheld and continue at the exact same point on your tablet or phone.
